### Description Text:

What happens when an enterprise receives a supplier invoice that doesn't match what was ordered? 

Traditional accounts payable software can calculate the arithmetic variance and flag an exception. But detecting a discrepancy is only half the problem. The hardest part is deciding what to do next: Has this supplier billed this way before? Did an authorized manager negotiate a waiver? And most importantly—is that historical precedent still valid under current company procurement policy?

Meet LedgerMind: a policy-aware accounts payable decision-support system that combines deterministic three-way matching with persistent institutional memory powered by Hindsight, version-governed policy evaluation, Groq-hosted LLM reasoning, and human-in-the-loop review.

Central Innovation: Policy vs. Precedent
LedgerMind never blindly copies historical decisions. When an institutional precedent conflicts with governing corporate policy, LedgerMind prioritizes policy compliance, flags the discrepancy, and provides explainable evidence to help human reviewers make consistent, accountable decisions.
